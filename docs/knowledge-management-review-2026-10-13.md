# Knowledge Management Review

Date: October 13, 2026

## Purpose

Review current approaches for capturing, organizing, and maintaining knowledge gained through learning activities, observations, and documentation efforts.

## Findings

- Documentation remains a valuable long-term reference.
- Lessons learned should be captured while recent activities are still fresh.
- Structured records improve consistency and future reusability.
- Continuous learning benefits from organized knowledge repositories.

## Outcome

Knowledge management practices remain active and continue to support future development activities.
